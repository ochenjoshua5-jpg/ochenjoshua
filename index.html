03

why i started apex.

apex started because i kept seeing the same thing — business owners i knew working twenty hours a day doing work a smart system could do in twenty minutes. and i knew i could build that system.

i didn't start apex because of some pitch deck or a trend. i started it because i've watched people in my community burn out doing manual work that shouldn't be manual. replying to the same whatsapp messages. chasing the same leads. copying the same data into the same spreadsheets. day after day.

apex is me saying no to that. it's the thing i always wanted to build — ai systems that handle the boring stuff so real people can focus on the parts of their business that actually need a human.

every product inside apex is something i personally wish existed when i was helping those shops and clinics. customer support bots. lead follow-up. workflow automation. tools that buy people back their time.

apex is a 19 year old's answer to a problem that's been here forever. and it's working.

Ochen Joshua (Wealth)

Ochen Joshua — also written Joshua Ochen and known publicly as Wealth — is a Ugandan software engineer, entrepreneur and DJ based in Kampala, Uganda. He is the founder of Apex Automation Solutions and the creator of OJ MUSIC and OJ Music Downloader.
